// LLM Intelligence

50 generation evolution. 4 phases. Natural selection.

Every cycle runs 50 generations through four phases: 10 MOMENTUM_SQUEEZE, 10 STRUCTURE, 10 GEOMETRIC, then 20 HONE_BEST that mutates the top-scoring candidate from the first three phases. Powered by Gemini 3.1 with a 2.5 Flash, Flash-Lite, and Claude Opus 4.7 fallback chain. It ingests sentinel broadcasts, the strategic digest, the shadow matrix of missed trades, the full indicator physics from every recent win and loss, and persistent cross-cycle memory - past failures, paradigm performance stats, and meta-rules derived from what has gone wrong. Armed with 97 pre-computed indicator columns and a pure-numpy market-structure library (swing detection, BOS/CHoCH, order blocks, FVG consequent encroachment, volume profile, squeeze confluence). Candidates mutate a working paradigm template rather than reinventing from scratch. Successful candidates that compute synthetic indicators on-the-fly generate recommendations to add them to Streamline permanently. Then it evolves.

PHASE 1 · MOMENTUM / SQUEEZE
F SQUEEZE FIRE CONFLUENCE
Squeeze release as trigger, quality-filtered on 6+ bar coil duration and compression ratio below 0.5, then confluence-gated by ADX > 22 and DI spread direction. Only high-quality coils fire - dud squeezes are suppressed.
PHASE 2 · STRUCTURE
B ORDER BLOCK RECLAIM
Pure numpy detection: last opposing candle before a 1%+ displacement swing. Enter on retest of the OB zone in the correct premium/discount half of the range, with volume expansion > 1.2× the 20-bar average.
PHASE 2 · STRUCTURE
C PDL/PDH SWEEP RECLAIM
Previous Day Low or High sweep-and-reclaim using pre-computed calendar pivots. Low-ADX ranging filter (ADX_14 < 25) prevents firing in trends where sweeps rarely reclaim. Ranging regime specialist.
PHASE 2 · STRUCTURE
E BREAK OF STRUCTURE
Close above the last 50-bar swing high (or below swing low) as the BOS trigger, confluence-gated by ADX > 22 and volume expansion > 1.3×. Classic trend-continuation entry on confirmed structural break.
PHASE 3 · GEOMETRIC
A WYCKOFF SPRING
Price wicks below a 200-bar range low then closes back above it - the classic Wyckoff accumulation spring. Confluence-gated by RSI_14 < 35 (oversold). Longs only; shorts suppressed in ranging regime.
PHASE 3 · GEOMETRIC
D FIBONACCI OTE
Optimal Trade Entry zone: 61.8-78.6 percent retracement of the dominant 100-bar swing. Confluence-gated by ADX > 20 and DI spread > 5 in the entry direction. Institutional re-accumulation window.
PHASE 4 · HONE BEST (gens 31-50)
★ MUTATE THE TOP SCORER
After the first 30 generations across MOMENTUM_SQUEEZE, STRUCTURE, and GEOMETRIC phases, the top-scoring candidate becomes the HONE parent. Gens 31-50 take the parent's full source and tighten one threshold, swap one filter, or add one confluence check per generation. Architecture preserved, edges sharpened. This is where the swarm stops exploring and starts converging.
THE EVALUATOR
7-day walk-forward evaluation across 88 perpetual futures symbols with realistic taker fees (0.06%) and slippage (0.02% per side). Every generation is scored on overall performance AND on each of 3 rolling 2-day sub-windows - a strategy that is profitable overall but breaks on any sub-window gets halved. Honest Physics enforced: T-120 lookups with a 120-second delay so lookahead bias is impossible. Hard 1.2 ATR stop floor. 4-hour maximum hold time. Target 50-264 trades per cycle; above the ceiling a multiplicative over-trading penalty applies. Absolute PnL floor - negative net equals score zero regardless of win rate. Drawdown penalty - above 8% max drawdown deducts $2,000, above 15% deducts $5,000. Per-symbol quality - if fewer than 30% of symbols are profitable, score is halved. A peer-reviewer statically vets every candidate before eval time is spent. Operator-set safety rails. Non-negotiable.
STRICT BEAT OR HOLD
A new candidate must strictly beat the reigning champion on the same fresh 7-day window or the throne stays. Champion and runner-up both saved to the Hall of Fame, so every surviving strategy remains available for analysis. If nothing beats the live config, the system holds. No change for the sake of change. The swarm evolves only when evolution produces something genuinely superior - and proves it holds up across every walk-forward sub-window.
