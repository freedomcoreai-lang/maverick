About

It never sleeps.
It never stops mutating.

MAVERICK is alive. It scans 88 crypto futures markets every sixty seconds, and a single evolved champion owns every decision on the book. Which symbol to enter. How much to risk. Where the stop goes. When to trail. When to exit. No human approves a trade. No human sets a stop. No human picks a symbol. The champion fought its way to the throne, and the champion runs the capital.

But the champion is not forever. Every few days an LLM swarm rewrites the DNA from the ground up, using the corpses of losing trades as teaching material. The strategy trading right now has an expiry date. The one that replaces it may look nothing like it.

Execution is the easy part. The real machine is what happens between trades.

Every loss is dissected. The system watches itself die, studies the tape, and tags the structural defect for the next cycle. The Matrix reads the full state of the market and turns evidence into forensics. Every forty eight hours a multi-tier council runs a fresh Swarm: a panel of NVIDIA-hosted scouts proposes candidates, a bench of premium frontier models deliberates across rounds, and a Supreme Court synthesises the verdict. It performs root cause analysis on every losing pattern, proposes new DNA, validates the candidate against six days of real market data, and only deploys if the challenger strictly beats the sitting champion. The strategy that trades tomorrow may not be the same strategy that traded yesterday. It evolves. It forgets what no longer works. It remembers what killed it.

85,000 lines of production Python. 128 million backtest evaluations logged. Seven autonomous AI agents looping forever. A Sovereign Live Adapter refreshing the in memory state every five minutes and gating every candidate through a contract validator before it touches a dollar of live capital. Honest Physics enforced. T minus sixty on every lookup. No lookahead. No theory.

Nothing on this website is simulated. Every number you read came from real markets, real trades, and real capital at risk. You are not watching a demo. You are watching a self-evolving AI bot hunt.

The System

The Sovereign Hybrid
One bot. One champion. Self-rewriting every cycle.

A single reigning champion owns everything: entry logic, sizing, leverage, stops, trails, exits. It runs across all 88 perpetual futures, ticking every second. The swarm evolves new candidates continuously. Only a strategy that strictly beats the live champion on a fresh 6-day honest-physics backtest takes the throne. If nothing beats it, the system holds. No change for the sake of change. The current apex is loading…, and the swarm is actively honing its successor right now.

🎯
Live Champion
The reigning live strategy. Reads the live feature set and fires entries the moment its conditions line up, across all 88 perpetual futures. A 2.5 ATR initial stop protects capital, a two-stage volatility trail ratchets profits as the move extends, and a 4-hour time-exit closes any position that fails to work. When the swarm evolves a strategy that strictly beats this one on an honest-physics backtest, the atomic hot-swap pipeline replaces it live - and this card updates the moment it does.
⚙
The Unified Engine
Evaluator and live bot run the same code path. One Python module (maverick_core) routes signals, sizes positions, and manages exits for both backtest and live. The parity gate is locked at zero percent divergence. This kills the oldest class of trading-bot bug: "it worked in backtest but fails live." If the champion scores well in evaluation, it behaves identically on the exchange.
🔒
Sovereign Live Adapter
Auto-refreshes the champion's in-memory cache every 5 minutes so live state mirrors the evaluator's. Every new candidate passes the Champion Validator - DNA schema check, contract correctness on pre_filter / signal_entry / manage_position, idempotent cache guarantees, and the 2.5 ATR stop-floor. The atomic hot-swap runs a 45-second live health check on every deploy and rolls back on failure. No bifurcation between what evaluation saw and what fires on the exchange.
🧹
The God-Mode Swarm
A federated council of frontier AI sits behind every champion. Stage A scouts - a panel of fast, specialised models hosted on NVIDIA - draft candidate strategies from a blank scaffold, anchored to market structure on every thesis. Stage B councilors - the premium bench of frontier reasoning models - debate the candidates across rounds, surfacing assumptions, edge cases and honest-physics violations. The Supreme Court synthesises a binding verdict. Candidates are rejected if they duplicate a prior thesis, violate the payoff-ratio floor, or crack under T-120 lookahead detection. Every new frontier model release drops into the relevant tier on the next cycle. You are not betting on one mind. You are running ten-plus of the best minds available on Earth, debating in chambers, and only the verdict touches your money.
