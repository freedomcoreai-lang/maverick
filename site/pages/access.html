// Frequently Asked

Is this a security? Am I buying a share in MAVERICK?
No. Both rails grant access to a software service. You receive no equity, no dividends, no share of trading profits, no promise of returns. You are paying for coverage of the experiment.
Crypto or subscription - which should I pick?
If you already have a Base wallet, $MAV is simpler long-term and your tier holds whatever happens to MAV's price. If you don't touch crypto, the $29-$199/mo subscription is identical access, billed by Stripe. Both unlock the same tiers. Annual saves 33% on either rail.
Why Base and not Solana or Ethereum?
Base is a Coinbase L2. Fees are fractions of a cent. MetaMask and Coinbase Wallet both support it natively. Ethereum mainnet is too expensive for tier-access tokens. Solana is culturally meme-heavy - we're infrastructure, not memes.
How does the site know my tier?
Crypto rail: you sign a free off-chain message when connecting. The server reads your wallet's $MAV balance directly from Base. Tier auto-updates within 60 seconds of any buy or sell. Fiat rail: your email and active subscription status from Stripe determine tier; updates instantly on webhook events.
What makes this not a rug?
Contract ownership renounced at deploy. Minting permanently disabled at deploy. Liquidity locked 24 months via Team Finance. Team allocation vests 24 months on-chain. Every treasury movement is publicly verifiable on BaseScan. Stripe subscriptions are managed by Stripe, not us - we can't charge you in error.
Where can I buy $MAV?
Once launched, on Aerodrome (Base's native DEX). A direct buy button will appear here when the pool is live. Until then, connect your wallet now to claim free pre-launch Observer access.
